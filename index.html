🧻 Chope Master

A Singapore hawker centre survival game

Chope a table, order food, defend your tissue from wind, pigeons and cats, and eat in peace!

  1. Chope an empty table — walk up, press E.
  2. Head to the stall, press E to queue.
  3. Wind will just take your tissue — but a pigeon can be shooed off! Get close and throw a shoe (F) before it grabs, or hit it as it flies away to knock the tissue loose.
  4. A cat may run off with it too — chase it and stay close to force it to drop the tissue, or it'll get away for good.
  5. Dropped tissue packets sit on the ground for a bit — walk up and press E to grab them back before they blow away.
  6. Table gone? Run back and chope again, fast.
  7. Grab your food, bring it back, press E to eat.

Move: WASD/Arrows   Sprint: Shift   Interact: E   Throw shoe: F
