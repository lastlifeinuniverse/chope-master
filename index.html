🧻 Chope Master

A Singapore hawker centre survival game

Help Cherie chope a table, order her meal, defend the tissue packet from wind and birds, and eat in peace!

  1. Walk to an empty table and press E to chope it with a tissue packet.
  2. Head to the stall and press E to join the queue.
  3. Watch out — wind and birds may steal your tissue while you wait!
  4. If it's gone, run back and chope again before someone takes your table.
  5. Collect your food when ready, bring it back, and press E to sit and eat.

Move: WASD/Arrows   Sprint: Shift   Interact: E
